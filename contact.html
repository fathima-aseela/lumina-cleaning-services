10 Essential Tips for a Cleaner, Healthier Home

May 20, 2024
by Lumina Team
8 min read

A clean home is more than just visually appealing—it promotes better health, reduces stress, and improves overall well-being. Here are 10 practical tips to help you maintain a cleaner, healthier home every day.

1. Start with a Cleaning Schedule

Consistency is key! Create a weekly cleaning schedule to stay on track and avoid last-minute deep cleans.

2. Use Eco-Friendly Cleaning Products

Switch to non-toxic, eco-friendly cleaning products that are safe for your family and pets.

→ Explore our Eco-Friendly Cleaning Services

3. Declutter Regularly

Less clutter means less dust and dirt. Donate or discard items you no longer need.

4. Sanitize High-Touch Surfaces Daily

Door handles, light switches, faucet taps, and remote controls accumulate thousands of microbes daily. Wipe them with a botanical disinfectant at the end of each evening.

5. Improve Indoor Air Quality with HEPA Filtration

Vacuum regularly using HEPA filter vacuums to trap micro-allergens, pet dander, and fine dust particles instead of recirculating them back into the air you breathe.

6. Wash Bedding & Linens Weekly in Hot Water

Wash pillowcases and bed sheets in water at 60°C (140°F) to eliminate dust mites and skin oils, keeping sleeping areas fresh and sanitary.

7. Adopt a Shoes-Off Policy Indoors

Over 80% of household floor bacteria is tracked in from outside shoes. Place a comfortable shoe rack near your entranceway to protect hardwood and carpets.

8. Deep Clean Kitchen Sponges & Microfiber Cloths

Microwave damp sponges for 60 seconds or wash microfiber cloths in bleach-free detergent to prevent cross-contamination across countertops.

9. Manage Moisture to Prevent Mold Growth

Run exhaust fans during showers and wipe down glass shower screens immediately after use to maintain humidity levels below 50%.

10. Schedule Quarterly Professional Deep Cleanings

Even with diligent daily habits, hard-to-reach areas, baseboards, upholstery, and deep grout lines benefit greatly from specialized commercial equipment and trained cleaning specialists.
